How credits work

Monthly allowance first. Purchased credits second.

1. Monthly included allowance Your plan's included conversations are consumed first each billing cycle.
2. Purchased top-up credits Any additional packs remain available and are used only after the included allowance is exhausted.
3. Buy more or upgrade When capacity runs out, merchants can choose the right next step instead of facing confusing billing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions merchants usually ask first.

What counts as a recovery conversation?

One recovery conversation is one abandoned checkout entering Moda Interact's automated WhatsApp recovery flow.

Do purchased top-ups expire?

No. Purchased conversation credits are intended to remain available until they are used.

Why do top-ups change by plan?

Subscribers are committing to a monthly relationship, so they receive stronger pricing on additional conversation packs.

Do I pay Meta separately?

No. The intention is a simpler merchant experience without separate WhatsApp billing complexity.

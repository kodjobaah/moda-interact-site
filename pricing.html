Proposed credit definition

One credit per admitted proactive outreach attempt

The report's proposed unit is an admitted proactive outreach attempt, not each WhatsApp reply. An eligible follow-up uses another credit; customer replies and in-window AI or merchant responses do not use another credit under the proposal. This does not verify or change current runtime behaviour.

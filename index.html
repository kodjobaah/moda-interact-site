Product

Built for merchants that want faster, smarter customer engagement.

Moda Interact is a software-as-a-service platform designed to connect e-commerce workflows with messaging channels. It helps merchants automate customer outreach, engage shoppers at the right time and streamline communication journeys.

Shopify integration

How it works

From store event to customer conversation.

Moda Interact is being built to help merchants bring storefront activity, messaging and automation together in one workflow.

1. Commerce event
Shopify triggers an event such as an abandoned checkout or customer action.
2. Workflow logic
Moda Interact applies the configured merchant workflow and engagement rules.
3. Messaging
The platform sends or manages the appropriate customer communication.
4. Ongoing engagement
Responses can continue through AI-assisted or structured conversational flows.

What Moda Interact aims to do

Moda Interact is focused on helping businesses automate customer communication in a way that is relevant, timely and connected to e-commerce activity.

The platform is intended to support use cases such as abandoned-cart recovery, customer follow-up, message-triggered workflows and AI-assisted support interactions.
